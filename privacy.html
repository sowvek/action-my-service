1. Who we are

Action My Service offers web development services and information about possible digital/AI tool subscription options. The business responsible for your personal information is Action My Service (legal business name not yet provided). Contact: Business email to be added before publication.

2. Information we may process

  • Inquiry information: name, contact details, selected service and the details you choose to include in a message.
  • Transaction and project information: requirements, agreed deliverables, correspondence and payment or invoice information if you become a customer.
  • Technical information: web hosting and security infrastructure may process IP address, browser or device data, request times and error logs.

3. How the contact form works

On this starter site, the inquiry form prepares a message in your browser. It does not submit your details to a website database or a server-side form service. You can copy your message, or, if the owner configures those options, open your email application or WhatsApp and decide whether to send it. If you send a message, it will be processed by the relevant communication service and by us after receipt.

4. Why we use information

  • To respond to inquiries and provide requested quotes or service information.
  • To negotiate, deliver and support services you agree to purchase.
  • To issue invoices, maintain business records and meet applicable legal obligations.
  • To protect against abuse, fraud and security incidents.

Depending on the laws that apply, our lawful basis may be taking steps at your request before a contract, performing a contract, complying with legal duties or pursuing legitimate business interests. We seek consent where required.

5. Service providers and disclosure

When the website is hosted on Vercel, that hosting provider may process technical data needed to serve and protect it. If you contact us through email or WhatsApp, the provider of that communication service processes data under its own policies. We may use carefully selected third-party services to deliver an agreed customer project, subject to the relevant agreement. We do not claim to sell inquiry data to advertisers.

6. Cookies and analytics

The starter website does not include advertising pixels, tracking analytics, a cookie banner, or deliberate browser storage. This can change if the owner adds tools or integrations. See our Cookie Policy.

7. Retention and security

Inquiry data that you actually send to us is retained only as long as reasonably necessary for correspondence, work, legitimate records and any applicable legal duties. No online or messaging system can be guaranteed completely secure. Please do not include account passwords, one-time codes, government identification or sensitive financial details in an inquiry.

8. Your privacy choices

Depending on where you live and the applicable laws, you may have rights to request access, correction, deletion, restriction or objection, or to withdraw consent where relevant. Contact us using the business contact information above to make a request. We may need to verify a request before responding.

9. International services and children

Hosting and communication providers may process information in other countries under their applicable safeguards. Our services are not designed for children to purchase independently; parents or guardians should make inquiries for minors.

10. Changes

We may update this policy when our site, services, data practices or legal obligations change. The updated date indicates the current version.
