Accessibility Statement

Our approach to making Action My Service easier to use for as many visitors as possible.

Last updated: 19 October 2026

1. Our approach

We aim to provide a readable, keyboard-friendly and responsive experience. This starter site includes semantic headings, visible focus styles, labels for form inputs, a skip-to-content link, and a preference for reduced motion.

2. Current limitations

We have not commissioned a comprehensive independent accessibility audit and do not claim verified compliance with a particular WCAG level. Third-party messaging services and user-supplied media may have their own limitations.

3. Need assistance?

If you encounter a barrier, please contact us and describe the page, feature, device or assistive technology involved. We will review practical improvements. Business contact information needs to be configured before publication.

4. Ongoing improvements

Accessibility is an ongoing effort. Features and content may change over time, and we welcome specific feedback that helps improve the experience.
