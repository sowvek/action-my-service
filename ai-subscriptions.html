AI Subscription Disclosure

Important details about independent third-party AI subscriptions, eligibility, prices and safe access.

1. Independent service brand

Action My Service is an independent digital services brand. References to AI platforms or other third-party products describe the kind of tools customers may ask about. They do not imply that we are partnered with, endorsed by or an authorized reseller of any specific platform. Any such status must be verified and explicitly stated for the relevant product before sale.

2. Availability and licensing

Whether an AI plan can be offered, transferred, resold, shared or provisioned depends on the provider's contract and the customer's eligibility. Some providers prohibit account transfers, shared logins, commercial resale or access outside a licensed region. We do not offer or recommend arrangements that require violating a provider's terms. Before listing any particular product, the business owner must verify that the exact proposed access arrangement is authorized.

3. What to confirm before paying

  • Exact provider and plan name, plus the subscription period.
  • What features, usage limits, seats and regions are included.
  • Whether the plan is an official license, an authorized resale or another permitted arrangement.
  • Total cost, renewal rules, support and cancellation/refund conditions.
  • How the customer receives access without exposing personal credentials.

4. Account and payment safety

Never share your main account password, one-time verification code or private API keys in an inquiry. Use the official provider's supported access and team features wherever available. Do not send payment to an unverified personal account.

5. Third-party changes

Providers can change pricing, features, availability and usage policies. Any specific entitlement or remedy must be outlined in your actual order and remains subject to applicable law.

6. No guaranteed provider relationship

Unless written proof for a particular offering is provided, no connection with a named AI company should be inferred. Third-party trademarks and product names belong to their respective owners.
