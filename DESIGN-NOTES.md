# Design notes — Action My Service

A deliberate departure from the previous dark/glowing template. Studio Edition uses an editorial warm-paper canvas, oversized near-black headlines, a small italic serif contrast, generous whitespace, fine rules, and limited accent colours from the supplied original logo.

- Primary ink: `#151719`
- Page background: `#f6f5ef`
- Lime accent: `#cde25b`
- Orange accent: `#f6a256`
- Blue accent: `#8dbce5`
- Layout: generous 1380px container, asymmetric hero, fine section dividers
- Typography: bundled system sans-serif + classic serif accents, no font files or tracking request
- Demo visuals: HTML/CSS illustrative concepts, **not** real customer case studies

Edit styles in `assets/styles.css`, pages in `.html`, and business contact details in `assets/config.js`.
