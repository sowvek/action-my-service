Refund & Cancellation Policy

How refunds and cancellation requests are handled for custom website work and eligible digital subscriptions.

Last updated: 19 October 2026

1. Please review your order first

Before any purchase, ask for a written confirmation of the specific service, price, delivery schedule, revision limits, support arrangements and applicable cancellation or renewal terms. Do not assume a subscription is refundable or renewable without checking the particular plan.

2. Website project cancellations

If you wish to cancel a website project, contact us as soon as possible. Any refund, partial refund or outstanding amount will depend on the work already completed, agreed milestones, nonrecoverable third-party costs, the written project agreement and mandatory consumer laws. A project that has not begun may be treated differently from one already in design or development.

3. Digital and AI subscription orders

Cancellation, renewal and refund eligibility for digital subscriptions depend on the specific plan, applicable provider rules, the service already supplied, your order confirmation and consumer protection law. We will explain any material restrictions before a paid order is confirmed. We do not promise universal refunds or impose a universal no-refund rule.

4. Problems with delivery or access

If a website deliverable does not match the agreed scope, or an eligible digital plan cannot be delivered as agreed, contact us promptly with your order details. We will review the issue and discuss an appropriate resolution, which may include correction, alternative delivery or a refund where required or agreed.

5. How to request cancellation or a refund

Use the verified business email once published, and provide your name, order reference, date, service, a short description of the issue and the outcome requested. Never send passwords, security codes or payment card details in a message.

6. Payment timelines and statutory rights

If a refund is agreed or legally required, processing time may depend on the payment method or provider. Nothing in this policy limits non-waivable rights under applicable law. Specific cancellation windows and business details must be completed before the site is used to accept paid orders.
