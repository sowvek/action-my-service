1. Current cookie use

As delivered, this static website does not intentionally set cookies or use localStorage/sessionStorage. It also does not load advertising tags, third-party analytics scripts, embedded videos or external font services. Its inquiry form only prepares a message locally in the browser.

2. Hosting and communication providers

Website infrastructure providers, including Vercel if selected for hosting, may use technical measures for security, abuse prevention or delivery. Opening a separate email app or WhatsApp link takes you to services governed by their own policies. We cannot control every provider's cookies or technical logging.

3. If the website changes

If we add analytics, payment integrations, embedded content, advertising or other optional technologies, this policy will need to be revised. Where applicable law requires it, appropriate notice and a working cookie consent mechanism must be added before optional tracking runs. We do not display a misleading cookie consent banner when the starter site itself has no optional cookies.

4. Managing cookies

You can clear or block cookies in your browser settings. Some future site features or external services may depend on cookies or similar storage.
