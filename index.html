05 / QUESTIONS, ANSWERED

The things
you might be
wondering.

Still have a question? You can always ask us directly.

What kind of website can you make? +

We can discuss business websites, portfolios, landing pages, catalogs, stores and redesign projects. The exact pages, integrations and functionality are set out in an agreed quote.

How much does a website cost? +

Every project has different needs. Share what you're looking for and we'll quote based on the pages, features, content, design and timeline involved.

Can you build a website like my reference? +

References are welcome! We'll use them to understand your taste and needs, then create a design suited to your business rather than copying someone else's protected work.

Which AI tool subscriptions are available? +

Options vary over time. Tell us the product and plan you're interested in. We'll confirm whether an authorized, terms-compliant offer is available and explain the cost and restrictions before payment.

Do you offer support after a website is live? +

Handover, revisions and post-launch support are defined in your quote. We can discuss ongoing maintenance separately if it is available for your project.

What about cancellations and refunds? +

They depend on the agreed work, project stage, applicable law and any third-party terms. Please read the Refund & Cancellation Policy before making a purchase.
